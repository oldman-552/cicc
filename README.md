## 2.2 Core Architecture: Five Intelligence Centers

Organize the platform into five interconnected environments.

### CENTER 1 — INTELLIGENCE

Centralize global cyber intelligence collection, monitoring, correlation, and threat awareness.

### CENTER 2 — RESEARCH

Centralize vulnerabilities, exploits, PoCs, repositories, technical research, papers, datasets, and security knowledge.

### CENTER 3 — DEFENSE

Centralize authorized asset monitoring, exposure management, security posture, detection engineering, incident analysis, and defensive operations.

### CENTER 4 — AI COMMAND

Centralize AI agents, investigation workflows, intelligence fusion, forecasting, quality assurance, simulation, and decision support.

### CENTER 5 — 3D UNIVERSE

Provide an immersive 3D environment for navigating intelligence entities, relationships, investigations, infrastructure, campaigns, and historical events.

All five centers must share one unified data model, permission system, search engine, notification system, audit framework, and intelligence knowledge graph.
